# Bounded truck-layer extraction

Canonical reusable implementation: [extract_blue_strict.py](extract_blue_strict.py). Source clips and earlier candidates stay intact. [track_cab.py](track_cab.py) supplies observed source anchors before extraction. [finish_blue.py](finish_blue.py) applies reversible rigid seam translations and creates the observed tracking for retimed output.

Input convention: city1505×1045, scaled to height720 (width1037) and centered at x121..1158 in provider1280×720. Preserve this full camera; local crop jobs require their recorded inverse placement first. Composite only onto the exact empty map.

1. Motion gate: native quarter-second source frames, correct corner and leading cab, one connected rigid trailer, no swaps, wrong streets, reverse, duplicate or roof crossing. Driver/head-count remains a separate visual uncertainty.
2. Track actual cab pixels in source: temporal difference, color-specific hue/saturation, predicted25px local window and saturation weights. Seed extraction from this source track. Largest unrestricted blue components are unsafe: blue street/rear wheel pixels caused demonstrated50–64 /31–74 frame cab losses.
3. Per-clip path ROI and allowed road rectangles. Temporal median + MAD; restore persistent WHITE trailer pixels from exact clean-map blue road ONLY where temporal variation proves the pixel changes. Restored threshold25. White crosswalk bands may still split the trailer; unite collinear moving white fragments behind the cab and fill their white/gray convex core. This repaired a demonstrated missing rear half. A separate neutral-white support extends10px upward/8px downward and6px sideways from the trailer core to bridge the measured front-roof/cab gap; source color is retained and cream pixels are excluded. Check this support against actual roadside white markings before reusing it.
4. Cab opaque core is the convex hull of nearby actual moving cab-color pixels. Retain original source RGB; do not paint bodies/drivers. White trailer core and dark wheels/hitch use connected temporal difference with compact hull support. Exclude saturated city greens and cream outside cab.7px white opening limits thin static attachments. Feather1px; neutral BLACK shadow from measured relative darkening, bounded below/right (no copied blue asphalt shadow).
5. Validate all frames: RGBA121, dimensions1280×720, source cab center opaque every frame, white-trailer/whole-body area floors without collapses; then visually review quarter-second alpha frames and native source/layer/composite strips. Counts are a loss detector, not proof every mirror/wheel is perfect. Tiny neutral matte fringe may need conditional acceptance at1×; report it explicitly.
6. Seam corrections: translation ONLY of whole premultiplied RGBA layer, then unpremultiply; no rotation, scaling, deformation, ghosts or crossfades. Maximum24frame ramps. Use observed cab position and velocities; measure corrected step against ordinary steps. Cab-center road-mask distance after21px closing is an IMAGE clearance measure, not world wheel clearance.
7. Slow versions repeat held original poses at24fps, no opticalflow/generated poses. Tracking uses exactly the same source-frame mapping, plus exact rigid translations. Blue480→1092 frames =20→45.5 seconds. Review slow-motion judder and seams before final integration.

Red/yellow adaptation: replace the cab hue/saturation predicate and tracker initial centers/ROIs, keep source tracking and white-trailer repair. Green or blue thresholds must not be reused for red/yellow. Red hue wraps at0/180 in OpenCV. Preserve the current paid-source clips and camera placement. Never infer driver count from prompt text or recoloring.
