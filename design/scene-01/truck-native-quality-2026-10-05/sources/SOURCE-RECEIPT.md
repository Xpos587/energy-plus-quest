The photo is an author's assembled physical print, not a validation render of the imported source.

IMPORTANT: STL parts are in print orientations and have numbered filenames; importing all at the origin does not assemble the truck. No rig/materials are included. Reassemble and assign materials before rendering; add glass mesh to the windshield and two drivers. The body part `r730-1.stl` has measured dimensions 110.4 x 95.409 x 111.6 mm. The model's stated assembled length is 285 mm; derive scale only after assembly. `r730-46.stl` is another large cab-related part. Inspect those first. Geometry quality is suitable as a detailed source; assembly and Blender visual verification remain required.
