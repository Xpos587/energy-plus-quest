# Feedback V3 Artwork

Generated on 2026-08-20 with fal.ai model `fal-ai/recraft/v4.1/text-to-image`.

## Direction

- Flat editorial browser-game illustration with dry-brush gouache texture.
- Deep logistics blue, safety orange, warm white, and pale ice blue.
- Dark-blue hand-drawn contours, simplified friendly geometry, no photorealism.
- No generated logos, readable vehicle text, UI, pins, or decorative traffic.

## Carrier board

The desktop master requested one snowy logistics-yard scene containing exactly four vehicles in a shared camera, light, texture, and perspective: a worn smoking truck, a modest gray truck, a blue two-driver truck, and a clean orange truck. The final UI uses the complete generated scene with accessible HTML hotspots instead of compositing independently edited vehicle PNGs.

The mobile prompt used the same direction in a portrait composition with four separated tap zones. It constrained the image to exactly two visible people, both naturally seated in the blue crew truck. The pale painted fields in the generated art are used as the visual ground for short HTML labels.

## Professional

The selected professional prompt requested a stern male northern logistics foreman around 40 years old, with a weathered face, confident stance, and modern blue-and-safety-orange insulated PPE based on page 3 of `materials/feedback/2026-08-17/source.pdf`. It explicitly excluded office props, a clipboard, glasses, fake logos, text, photorealism, and cropped limbs.
