# Feedback V2 Outcome Artwork

Provider: fal.ai

Model: `fal-ai/recraft/v4.1/text-to-image`

Use case: `illustration-story`

Shared asset contract:

- Wide narrative backdrop for an interactive browser-game outcome.
- Flat editorial game illustration with softly textured gouache surfaces, rounded geometry, restrained detail, and warm human presence.
- Slightly elevated three-quarter perspective so the vehicle, road, people, and parcel read as one coherent scene.
- Palette: deep logistics blue, vivid orange, warm white, pale ice blue, muted northern dusk neutrals.
- Main action remains in the left and central 60% of the frame; the right 40% stays calmer for a readable story panel.
- No logos, no brand marks, no readable text, no watermark, no UI cards, no infographic circles, no collage treatment.
