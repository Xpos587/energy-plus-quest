# V20 game loop — 64 seconds

Canonical source: `v20-game-loop.blend`, generated by `build_from_routes.py` from `routes/motion.npy`.

- 1536 encoded frames at 24 fps, 1920 × 1334, fixed camera.
- Native frame 1537 is the closure witness for frame 1 and is **not** encoded.
- The green vehicle follows the outer circuit; blue slows near the warehouse. Red and yellow have opposite-phase periodic pace variation. Routes and timing are recorded in `routes/final64-report.json`.
- Fresh vehicle hulls were checked across all 1536 swept intervals; see `routes/fresh-hull-proof.json`.
- Current V19 city, compact rear axles, coherent tyre materials, trailer paint, city holdout depth, vehicle articulation, two green drivers, smoke and worn-cab suspension are retained.
- Wheel steering/rotation, smoke and cab suspension were rebaked across the entire loop; `loop-contract.json` records source boundary closure and the small wheel phase correction.
- Render uses the previously approved 8 TAA samples and 32 volume samples. `quality-comparison.png` compares the latter with 128 volume samples at the same pose.

`render_all.py` runs serial 48-frame vehicle and matching projected-shadow chunks. `compose_all.py` composes them over the branded city and produces `v20-game-loop-64s.mp4`; final encode validation is written to `validation.json`. `render-receipts.json` records process exits and measured timings.

`seam-render.json` compares independently rendered native vehicle frames 1 and 1537. Runtime tracking is exported separately from this exact saved source, not reused from earlier 8-, 48- or 96-second work.

Historical experiment files `build_loop.py`, `build.log`, `clearance-source.json`, and `quality-*` belong to the preliminary timing study. They are not the canonical final trajectories. Preliminary rendered frames were moved to `pilot-pre-route-vehicles/` before the final render began; no preliminary vehicle or shadow frame is reused in the game loop.
