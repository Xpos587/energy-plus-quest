## Recommended stance

Use Parnas as the spatial basis and Utkina for modern warehouse/dock proportions. Create a fictional civilian city informed by these relationships, not a literal site replica. Keep the warehouse loading yard visually distinct from the carrier-selection routes. Final palette/materials stay deferred to the designer.
