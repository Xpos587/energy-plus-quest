# Real warehouse references: spatial proposal, not approved style

Status: first user-review gate. This document constrains a technical study only. It does not approve rebuilding the scene or replacing game media.

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

A civilian warehouse district connected to a larger city. Recommended spatial basis: RAUM Parnas; supporting warehouse/dock proportions: Utkina Zavod. Actual photographs and World Imagery were inspected. No generative imagery.

## 2. Color

No final palette selected. Preserve existing game styling until designer references arrive. Neutral diagnostic geometry and distinct numbered route IDs are permitted only for checks; do not copy either warehouse's corporate colors.

## 3. Typography

Keep existing application typography. Reference review uses local Normalidad files and Georgia headings; this is document presentation, not a proposed game redesign. Truck numbers must remain readable independently of color.

## 4. Spacing & Grid

Borrow the observed hierarchy: vehicle, dock, warehouse, street, district. Do not treat listing floor area or photographic perspective as measured dimensions. Set and verify all model dimensions explicitly in the next approved technical iteration.

## 5. Layout & Composition

Recommended: warehouse plot to one side of the road network, one elongated primary building with a smaller office volume and a visibly bounded loading yard. External local street, neighboring blocks, more distant arterial; occupied urban fabric instead of the prior experiment's large empty center. This adapts observed structure, not literal cadastral geometry.

## 6. Components

Warehouse mass, repeated docks, low canopies, office volume, gate/fence, maneuvering yard, small service buildings, mixed-height neighboring blocks and green setbacks. No copied logos, signs or imagery as game textures. Omit unrelated industrial/energy infrastructure and the full highway interchange.

## 7. Motion & Interaction

All four selectable trucks remain outside the whole warehouse plot. Preserve source roles: 1/4 older and farther, 2 slow and near the office, 3 two drivers. The previous continuous near/far inequality was an experimental proxy, not literal source copy. Resolve office versus logistics-center references before freezing routes. Recheck full tractor/trailer swept envelopes, articulation, loops, visibility and numbers after geometry changes. No interactive-map UI redesign at this gate.

## 8. Voice & Brand

Original scenario copy and scoring remain authoritative. Explain observed facts separately from inferred game adaptations. Do not claim a map, a road addition or final style is approved by the user.

## 9. Anti-patterns

No four trucks circling a loading yard; no inflated empty block to force a distance metric; no tracing a satellite map as if it already supplied legal truck routes; no industrial megacomplex copied wholesale; no phone crop presented as a finished mobile composition; no production rollout of reference photos.
