## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept / sketch-to-render.
Create ONE polished landscape game illustration, a candidate for scene 2 of a Russian logistics educational game.
Reference 1 is the OLD MANUALLY DRAWN TRUCK PROTOTYPE: use its straightforward side-cutaway composition and four cargo situations as the structural foundation, not its crude vector execution.
Reference 2 warehouse and reference 3 river port are the STYLE authority: clean fine grey outlines, illustrated industrial objects, calm flat local colors, restrained soft cel shading, believable proportions, subtle tactile volume. Match that same illustrated world, NOT glossy CGI, NOT photorealism, NOT a childish toy truck.
Draw a long plausible articulated truck in Russian road context: compact blue cab-over tractor on RIGHT, trailer rear doors on LEFT, realistic trailer triple rear axle and tractor wheels correctly separate; no American long bonnet, no brand logos, no lettering. All truck fully inside canvas. Cargo bay dominates image. Background quiet very pale warm grey with minimal ground shadow, no landscape, UI, people.
CAMERA: true near-orthographic SIDE elevation of the cargo bay, horizontal floor and roof, rectangular front faces of cargo, NO isometric diagonal floor grid. Just very shallow bevels/top faces and contact shadows to retain the touch of 3D shared with the reference scenes. A deliberate polished 2.5D illustration with 2D gameplay readability.
CARGO layout: modular rectangular packing, visually coherent step-shaped/Tetris silhouette, four DISTINCT intentional empty spaces left to right, not random scattered parcels. Fill enough of the bay to make these usable voids legible; avoid a huge undifferentiated empty roof area. Do NOT add dividers or shelves between zones.
1 LEFT above the trailer rear wheels: sturdy wooden crates with clear kettlebell cargo pictogram, broad empty space directly above, NO restraint in this empty space.
2 LEFT-CENTER: one wide carton of fragile dishes with recognizable plate/glass pictogram, protective folded pale cloth over its top; clearly visible empty rectangular space directly above it, red wall restraint straps available at this location.
3 RIGHT-CENTER: a SMALL low recessed rectangular pocket between stacked parcels, a short red strap on its back wall. Exactly THREE small cartons stand in front of its lower entrance, visibly obstructing access. Make the pocket read as a shallow recess through its side walls and occlusion/contact shadows, not as just an empty slot sitting on top of three boxes. This is the ONLY local depth exception; overall view remains flat and readable. Do not hide the whole recess behind these cartons.
4 FAR RIGHT immediately behind cab, at trailer front wall: a clearly TALL narrow empty floor-to-upper-wall space where a long parcel could stand upright, red securing straps on wall; next to it a couple of BOXED pillows, using small pillow pictograms on cartons, NOT loose cushions on floor.
Preserve these scenario meanings. No gift placed yet. No decorative tubes or nonsensical axles. No numeric labels, dashed UI outlines, selection circles, arrows, legends, text or watermark: these will be added as real UI later. Output one finished wide image, not a comparison board.
